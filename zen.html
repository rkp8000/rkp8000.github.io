Outside the ever-changing shapes and categories of this world.


Outside the limitations of thought and the confines of conceptions.


Not the wave, but the ocean.


...beyond judgment...


...beyond fear...

Zen Challenges


The more you practice the more joyful and powerful your Zen will become.


Where can you find a minute of Zen?


Level 1: Doing the dishes.


Level 2: Reading the news.


Level 3: Surrounded by mosquitoes.


Level 4: In the heat of a difficult conversation.


Level 5: In the bitter cold.


Level 6: In deep thought.


Level 7: In great joy.


Level 8: In self-examination.


The secret of happiness is to reserve at least a minute per day for Zen.


If today you have only one minute, let it be the crown jewel.
